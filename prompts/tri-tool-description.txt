Does something on the owner's laptop. Set action to one of: find_file, focus_session, add_deadline, list_deadlines, finish_deadline, open_website, web_search, play_youtube, open_app, media_control, run_routine, set_timer, speak_on_laptop, take_screenshot, draft_email, directions, weather, take_note, read_notes, show_notes, system_status, lock_screen, send_notification, read_clipboard, save_clipboard, read_clips, show_clips, mute_mic, bluetooth_control, suspend_laptop, classroom_due, submit_assignment, confirm_submission, submission_status. Set input to what the owner wants, in English: for find_file the file or subject name like maths assignment or resume; for focus_session minutes and topic like 25 minutes for maths assignment; for add_deadline title | when, like maths project | tomorrow 5 pm or | in 2 minutes; for finish_deadline the title; for media_control play, pause, next, previous, volume up, volume down, volume 50 or mute; for mute_mic mute, unmute, or toggle; for bluetooth_control status, on, off, connect <device>, or disconnect; for send_notification title | message or message; for save_clipboard the text to save or none to save current clipboard; for run_routine work, study, chill, news or demo; for set_timer like 5 minutes for tea; for speak_on_laptop the words; for draft_email to | subject | body; for submit_assignment the assignment or class name, optionally | file name; otherwise the site, query, song, app, place or note. Use none when nothing is needed. Returns a sentence to speak to the owner. confirm_submission uploads the assignment, only after submit_assignment says go ahead or the owner says yes. submission_status says whether the upload is finished.
